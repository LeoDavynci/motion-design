## What I will not do
No invented metric (case study has none), no invented screens, no fake testimonials, no logo (none supplied: wordmark is typeset Geist).

## Open choices (defaults in bold)
1. Voice-over: **none, captions only** / ElevenLabs (needs key in .env).
2. Music feel: **warm, upbeat, mallet + sub** / more minimal.
3. Copy: **quoted from the case study** / you give new lines.
4. Screens in the add-person flow come at 1x from Figma (390x844). Close-ups may look slightly soft; I will re-export at 2x if possible, and use the live app for close-ups.

## Process
Build → contact sheet one frame per beat → score 1-10 on hook, phone-size readability, motion quality, variety, brand accuracy, sound sync → fix the 3 worst → repeat 3 rounds, all 8+ → full render (3600 sub-frame captures, ~20 min).
