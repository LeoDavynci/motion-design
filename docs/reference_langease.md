# Reference: "Best SaaS Product Launch Ad Video | LangEase" (33 s, 16:9)
Local copy for study only: refs/langease/ref.mp4, frame sheet refs/langease/sheet.png. Take technique, not subject, logo or UI.

## What it does
- **Pace:** something new every ~0.75-1 s. Short phrases, hard rhythm. Ours is a new idea every 3-5 s.
- **Type:** one phrase occupies one slot and the words swap in place with a blur ("Turn / Books / Audio", "Any language / Instantly"). Key words in the brand blue, the rest in dark. Medium weight, never huge.
- **UI in 3D:** product screens are tilted in real perspective and the camera flies through and between them. Cards float, tumble and orbit with soft shadows.
- **A pointer:** a cursor/hand glides to a button, clicks, and the UI reacts (a card lifts, a button presses).
- **A payoff beat:** a progress counter (86/100, 99/100) fills, then "Done" with a check and a burst of confetti.
- **Many copies of one thing:** a single card multiplies into a field of tilted cards ("Multiple Languages").
- **End card:** a sparkle shape resolves into the logo, then a three-word tagline with the first two words in blue ("Translate. Dub. Distribute").
- **Look:** near-white background with a lavender wash, blue gradients, soft long shadows, no dark mode.

## What I would apply to Peepl (needs your OK)
1. Real 3D perspective for phones (turn on the Y axis, camera fly-through between the map, the profile and Ask), instead of flat tilt.
2. In-place word swaps for the headlines, like "Names. → Stories. → Next steps."
3. A "20 s" timer that fills during capture, then a check and a small confetti burst on "Profile created". (The case study's goal is "add a person in under 30 seconds", so no invented metric.)
4. One profile card multiplying into a field of floating cards, one per moment or person ("Remember everyone").
5. Pointer/finger gliding to targets before each tap.
6. Sparkle → wordmark → "Capture. Ask. Act." end card, with the first words in the accent colour.
7. Faster cadence: cut the film from 8 beats to ~14, with every shot ≤ 2.5 s except the Ask and Moments flows.

## What I would NOT copy
Their palette (white and blue), their logo, the "zelios" watermark, stock footage, or any of their product screens.
