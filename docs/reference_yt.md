# Reference study: five SaaS launch films (refs/yt/*.mp4)

Contact sheets next to each file. Common moves:

1. **One idea per beat, 1-3 words.** "Too many / Too little", "Execute", "Deploy Smarter", "Tag", "User invited". Type swaps in place, never a paragraph.
2. **Type is scenery.** Giant cropped words ("SmartOps", "STREAMLINE", "Introducing") run behind or past the device at 30-60% of the frame. Applied: ghost words (PEOPLE / SPEAK / STORY / ASK) drifting behind the phone.
3. **Devices are planes in space.** Screens tilt, fan and stack in perspective instead of sitting flat. Already covered by the 3D phone and yawed cards.
4. **One colour world per film.** Purple (WMP, Soteria), green (Doks), one accent. Soft light blooms, no hard shapes. Matches our palette rules.
5. **Brand glyph as match cut.** The four-point star zooms until it fills the frame, then becomes the next scene. Our end-shot sparkle follows this.
6. **Completion gets a reward.** Check badge + confetti shards on "invited" / "complete". Already in the capture timer.
7. **Blur-through transitions.** Scenes dissolve through blur and push, never hard cuts.
8. **Ends clean.** Logo alone on a plain ground, one CTA line.
